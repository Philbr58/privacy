# Calendar – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 19 October 2026*

Calendar (`uk.philbrown.calendar`) is a calendar viewer and editor for Android phones, with a
companion app for Wear OS watches. It is published by philbrown.uk.

## Summary

Calendar does not collect, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics,
no advertising and no internet access of its own.

## Calendar data

The phone app reads and writes events through Android's built-in calendar storage (the
Calendar Provider), using the calendar permissions you grant. Those events belong to the
calendar accounts already on your phone, such as Google Calendar. Any syncing of those
accounts is done by Android and the account provider, not by this app.

## Watch companion

If you install the Wear OS app, the phone app sends a summary of upcoming events to your own
paired watch: calendar names and colours, and event titles, times, locations and shortened
descriptions. It uses the Wearable Data Layer in Google Play services. That is usually a
direct Bluetooth connection, but when the devices aren't directly connected Google Play
services may relay the data over an encrypted connection. The data is only delivered to watches
paired with your phone and signed into your account. It is never sent to the developer or
anyone else. In the other direction, the watch app tells the phone its version number and
the watch's device name, which the phone shows under Settings > About.

## Settings

Your display preferences, such as which calendars are shown and the terms you've hidden, are
stored on your device. If Android's backup is turned on, Android may include them in the
backup to your Google account, as it does for other apps.

## Permissions

- **Calendar (read and write):** to show your events and let you create, edit and delete them.
- **Network state, wake lock, run at startup, foreground service:** these come from Google Play
  services and Android's background-work library. They keep the watch summary up to date.

## Children

The app is not directed at children.
