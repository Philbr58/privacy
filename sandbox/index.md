# Sandbox – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 21 October 2026*

Sandbox (`uk.philbrown.sandbox`) is a test app for Android phones and Wear OS watches. It is
used by philbrown.uk to try out Google Play publishing. It is published by philbrown.uk.

## Summary

Sandbox does not collect, store, share or sell any data. It requests no permissions and has no
accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no internet access.

## What the app does

It shows its own version number and whether it was installed from Google Play. That
information is read from the device and never leaves it.

## Children

The app is not directed at children.
